Our rates are indicative

  • The national benchmark comes from the India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA), published on business days. IBJA's rates include import duty but exclude GST.
  • City rates come from local jewellers' associations or boards, named on each city page.
  • Futures prices come from MCX through a licensed data provider and may be delayed.
  • Prices at a shop, bank or lender include GST, making charges, wastage and margins, and will be higher.
  • Check the price with the seller before you pay. We are not responsible for decisions made using our rates.
2

Information, not investment advice

Kanak is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and is not regulated by the RBI. We do not tell you whether to buy, sell or hold gold or silver, and we do not predict prices. Past prices do not show what prices will do.

Gold ETFs and sovereign gold bonds are regulated securities. SEBI has said that "digital gold" products sold online are outside its regulation. Consider speaking to a registered adviser before investing.

Kanak is independent. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by IBJA, MCX, the Bureau of Indian Standards, or any jeweller, bank or lender. Names and trademarks belong to their owners and are used only to identify sources and businesses.
