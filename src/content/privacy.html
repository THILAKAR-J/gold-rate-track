How we protect it

We use encryption in transit, access controls, activity logs and backups, and we require the same of our service providers. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7

Your rights

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with.
  • Correct or update it, or erase it when we no longer need it or you withdraw consent.
  • Withdraw consent as easily as you gave it: reply STOP, use the unsubscribe link, or change Cookie settings.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or become unable to act.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

Write to [privacy@domain] or use the grievance form, giving the number or email you used with us. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We aim to reply within [30] days and will reply within 90 days.

Kanak is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and we do not track or target ads at children. If you think a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete their data.
